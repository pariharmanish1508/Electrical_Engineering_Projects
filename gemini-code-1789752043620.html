Power Saving Tips for a Greener & Lower Bill

  • Upgrade to 5-Star Appliances: Energy-efficient (Inverter ACs, 5-star refrigerators) consume up to 30-40% less electricity compared to older models.
  • Optimize AC Usage: Set your air conditioner temperature to 24°C instead of 18°C. Each degree higher saves roughly 6% on electricity.
  • Embrace LED Lighting: Swap legacy incandescent bulbs or old tube lights with high-lumens LED alternatives, saving more than 80% lighting power.
  • Unplug Standby Devices: Electronics consume phantom energy even when turned off. Unplug chargers, TVs, and microwaves when not actively in use.
  • Harness Natural Light & Air: Maximize cross-ventilation during pleasant hours to minimize continuous dependence on fans and coolers.
